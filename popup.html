Heads up about Penny Scan
Penny detection is an estimate based on public pricing and stock signals — it isn't a guarantee. Results may not be accurate: an item flagged as a penny may ring up at full price, be out of stock, or already be gone from the shelf.

Always verify the price at the register, and never assume a listed item is actually available or marked down.
